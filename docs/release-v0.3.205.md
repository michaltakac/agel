# v0.3.205

Transfer scoring verifies the donor as an adaptive run before crediting
its learning. A melee donor and recipient must agree on the training
policy, service policy, and optimizer phase. The scorer checks the
installed melee source against those manifest claims.

The launcher selects the melee entry point exposed by the pinned source
tree, including the earlier entry point used by older donors. A retained
one-rat transfer reproduced its previous score. Installed-source checks
accepted old and new melee manifests and rejected mismatched hashes and
phase claims.
