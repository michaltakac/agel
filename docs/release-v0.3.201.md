The adaptive three-skill melee benchmark starts optimization after
training Attack. The guest uses the live state and recorded workload
at that point, then continues with Strength and Defence on the same
connection. The fixed arm crosses the same transition without editing.

The comparison contract records the new phase boundary. A hosted
fixture verified that the callback runs once, sees the completed Attack
XP, and passes its returned state through the remaining two phases.
