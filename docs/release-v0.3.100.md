# v0.3.100

Agel benchmark results distinguish known costs, observed charges without
prices, and incomplete billing coverage. Ratios still require a successful
task and a positive known cost.

The first-sale scorer checks the expected cost field in the guest report
and independently requires the saved inventory to contain the pickaxe and
the exact sale proceeds. A leftover bar or ore invalidates the sale.

A hosted fresh-account run with the mining waypoint and retry logic
verified both ores and one smelted bronze bar at normal rights.
